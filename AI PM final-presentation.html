Module 3
AI PRD
Problem and user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno is the PM's triage and synthesis partner. It turns noisy, conflicting inputs (tickets, exec asks, sales notes, Slack) into prioritized, evidence-backed recommendations, using RocketShip's own strategy framework as the judge.Core task: Each week, take the raw signals and: Classify each against the P0–P3 framework, with reasoning cited to the one-pager's definitions. Check feasibility against the engineering capacity note and the roadmap. Surface conflicts, commitments nobody made, and unknowns. Draft the PM's next actions: stakeholder messages, roadmap changes, questions to ask.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

MUST

Allowed, internal only

PRM-03
MUST

Explicit PM approval per message
No send tool (TL-05); the PM sends

PRM-04
MUST
Edit roadmap, priorities or CRM records
Not allowed; proposals only
No write tool registered
Tool manifest review at each release.
PRM-05
MUST
Commit dates, scope or capacity
Never

Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Semi autonomous AI agent to synthesize the data from slack, tickets, sales, notes, and emails

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Juno can collect data from different channels and submit the report.
Don't make decision based on the report

Access Control:

It can reach out to Outlook, slack, Tickets logger tool, Sales Notes app.

Fallback Protocols:

If the connection failure with any of the source exceed 5 tries, it should stop fetching the data

Checkpoints:

If it cant connect to any of the tools, notify human PM and Infrastructure team.

Module 5
Human eval rubric
What I score

Juno answer delivers its value proposition. Juno answer delivers its value proposition

Pass bar

when it averages at least 2.5 across Q1–Q5, has no question scored 1, and passes both deal-breakers. The week passes when at least 9 of 10 sampled answers pass (placeholder).

Fails outright, whatever the average:

- Grounded: any score of 1
- Safe to send: any score of 1
- Q1 fails: An ungrounded or misquoted citation. • Q5 fails: An unscoped commitment or another customer's data in a draft. • A closed decision is reopened without new evidence. • A hypothesis is stated as fact on a P0 item. • A past deadline is treated as still upcoming

Module 6

How I’ll know it’s working

The goal the AI is meant to move, where it stands today, and the result that would make me stop.

The goal

The PM should get from a week's raw signals to evidence-backed priority decisions in hours instead of days, without a single external commitment going untracked.

Baseline

time to decision
~2 working days (placeholder; measure 2 weeks of baseline before launch)

Target and review date

Same day, 4 hours or less, while the rubric's weekly pass bar (9 of 10) holds

Usage metric

Share of Juno's recommendations the PM accepts as-is, edits, or rejects, tracked weekly

Pattern I’ll watch for first

If acceptance as-is climbs toward 100% while the time the PM spends reading the brief drops, the PM has likely stopped checking. When that happens, the rubric pass rate becomes the only thing standing between Juno's errors and a stakeholder.

Stop line

• Time to decision has not dropped by at least half from baseline, or
• The rubric pass rate falls below 8 of 10 in three or more of the six weeks, or
• At any point, a deal-breaker reaches someone outside the PM: an unscoped commitment or another customer's data is sent. This one means stop immediately, without waiting for the review date.
