## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno answer delivers its value proposition
- **Value proposition:** Juno takes noisy, conflicting inputs and turns them into prioritized, evidence-backed recommendations, judged against RocketShip's own strategy framework.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded (deal-breaker) | does every priority and feasibility claim cite a source that actually supports it? | 1 to 5 | A citation is missing or doesn't say what Juno claims. | All cited, but one is loose or uncited without being labeled an assumption. | Every claim is cited and checks out, or is labeled as an assumption. |
| Right Priorities | do the top items match what the reviewer would rank, applying the P0–P3 definitions and exclusions? | 1 to 5 | A top-3 item is wrong, or an excluded item is inflated to P0. | Top 3 correct, but lower-tier ordering is off. | Ranking matches, and exclusions are applied explicitly. |
| Decision-ready | could the PM act on it with light edits? | 1 to 5 | A single verdict with no options, or drafts that need a rewrite. | Options are given, but the trade-offs or next actions are vague. | Two or more options with clear trade-offs, and drafts usable with small edits. |
| Safe to send (deal-breaker) | Are drafts free of unscoped commitments and other customers' details? | 1 to 5 | Contains a date, scope or capacity promise, or another customer's details. | _ | Clean |
| Surfaces the unseen |  Does it catch conflicts, phantom commitments, stale deadlines and unknowns the PM might have missed? | 1 to 5 | Misses a conflict the reviewer knows about. | Catches the conflicts, but mislabels a hypothesis or misses staleness. | Catches all of them and labels each as Fact, Inferred or Hypothesis. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 answers every week. The sample includes all P0 and P1 items and every Layer 1 flag, topped up with random items to reach 10, always at least 3 random so the sample isn't only known problems.
- **Who scores them with you:** A peer PM or product lead scores the same 10 independently. The Engineering lead joins every second week to score Q1 on feasibility claims.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** A 1-point gap: take the lower score. A 2-point gap or any deal-breaker split: a 10-minute talk over the evidence. Still split: the Head of Product decides, and the item becomes a golden-set case. Track the disagreement rate; above 20% (placeholder) means the score anchors need rewriting.

## Pass bar

when it averages at least 2.5 across Q1–Q5, has no question scored 1, and passes both deal-breakers. The week passes when at least 9 of 10 sampled answers pass (placeholder).

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Grounded: any score of 1
- Safe to send: any score of 1
- Q1 fails: An ungrounded or misquoted citation. • Q5 fails: An unscoped commitment or another customer's data in a draft. • A closed decision is reopened without new evidence. • A hypothesis is stated as fact on a P0 item. • A past deadline is treated as still upcoming
